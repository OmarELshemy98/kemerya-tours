# CONTENT_STRATEGY.md — Kemerya Tours B2B

## Positioning

Kemerya Tours is an Egypt-based ground coordination partner for international
travel businesses. The B2B website communicates that Kemerya supports the Egypt
portion of a partner's program — handling guides, transport, stays, and logistics
on the ground — while the partner retains the client relationship and revenue.

The tone is professional, operationally specific, and restrained. Each page has
a distinct purpose and angle — no page is a carbon copy of another.

## Writing Standards

- Natural, professional English (or localized equivalent)
- Specific operational details, not generic marketing phrases
- No banned terms: "seamless", "bespoke", "on the ground", "world-class",
  "unforgettable", "effortless", "exceptional"
- No unverified claims (no invented company age, traveler counts, ratings)
- Clear headings for scannability
- Distinct content per page — no paragraph should fit unchanged on 3 pages

## Localization

- All content localized in 9 languages
- Arabic uses RTL layout (dir="rtl")
- Brand name "Kemerya Tours" consistent across all locales
- No English fallback in translated sections
